About
Us

We believe the best brands are built through experiences, not advertisements.

Studio Co Labs is a creative laboratory where strategy, culture, design and human connection come together to build brands people genuinely remember. With 12+ years in the LATAM Luxury Market.

We partner with ambitious companies to create experiences, campaigns and stories that live far beyond the moment. From intimate private dinners and product launches to social campaigns, content creation and cultural collaborations, every project is designed with intention and crafted to spark conversation.

We don't believe in creating noise. We believe in creating relevance.

Every brand has a story worth telling. Our role is to uncover it, shape it and bring it to life through thoughtful strategy, exceptional execution and meaningful connections.

Founded By

Fernando
Jaramuzchett.

Studio Co Labs was founded by Fernando Jaramuzchett, a brand strategist and experiential marketing consultant with more than a decade of experience creating campaigns, partnerships and luxury brand experiences across Latin America.

Throughout his career, Fernando has collaborated with global brands including ZEGNA, Tiffany & Co., Bottega Veneta, BVLGARI, Moët Hennessy, DIOR, L'Oréal Luxe and ERO, leading projects that connect brands with culture through meaningful experiences.

His work blends strategic thinking with hospitality, storytelling and community building, helping brands create moments that resonate long after the event has ended.

Privacy Notice

Privacy for clients.

This notice explains how Studio Co Labs and Fernando Jaramuzchett handle information shared by potential clients, collaborators and clients during inquiries, consultations and project conversations.

  • Information we collectWe may receive your name, company, role, email, phone number, WhatsApp messages, project brief, brand goals, budgets, timelines and any material you choose to share with us.
  • How we use itWe use this information to respond to inquiries, evaluate potential collaborations, prepare proposals, coordinate meetings, develop project strategy and deliver agreed services.
  • ConfidentialityProject details, brand plans, client context and consultation notes are treated as confidential business information and are not sold or publicly disclosed.
  • Service partnersWhen needed for a project, limited information may be shared with trusted production, creative, PR, venue, hospitality or technical partners who help us deliver the work.
  • RetentionWe keep inquiry and project information only as long as useful for communication, project records, legal/accounting needs or future collaboration context.
  • Your choicesYou may ask us to update or delete your contact information, or request that we stop contacting you about future opportunities.

For privacy questions or requests, contact Contact@studiocolabs.com or reach us through WhatsApp at +507 6960-4183.
